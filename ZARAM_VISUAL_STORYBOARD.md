# ZARAM VISUAL STORYBOARD

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Frozen (Production Authority)  
**Target Audience:** Cinematic Directors, Animation Programmers, Sound Designers  

---

## Sequence 1: Application Startup (The Awakening)
*   **Camera:** Starts in absolute black. Slowly pulls back on the Z-axis.
*   **Lighting/Animation:** A single, microscopic point of light appears at (0,0,0). Over 3 seconds, it expands into the Living Orb, lighting the volumetric fog.
*   **Particles:** The ambient data-dust fades in, drifting lazily.
*   **Sound:** A deep, resonant bass swell, tapering into a soft, ambient hum.
*   **Emotion:** Awe, potential, birth.

## Sequence 2: Searching (The Hunt)
*   **Camera:** Triggered by typing. The camera executes the Dolly Zoom (pulling back while narrowing FOV).
*   **Lighting:** The volumetric search-light cuts through the fog. All non-relevant objects drop to 5% opacity.
*   **Animation:** Relevant nodes (Documents, Tasks) detach from their standard orbits and glide swiftly along procedural splines to form a neat constellation in front of the camera.
*   **Sound:** The mechanical, satisfying clack of a heavy lock tumbling into place.
*   **Emotion:** Precision, command, clarity.

## Sequence 3: Entering a Project (The Descent)
*   **Camera:** The user double-clicks a Project monolith. The camera accelerates forward, FOV widening to 24mm.
*   **Lighting/Particles:** The particles stretch into motion blur. The global lighting matches the Project's specific emissive color code.
*   **Animation:** The Project monolith scales up massively, revealing its internal structure (sub-folders, tasks, documents) which unfold spatially like a geometric flower blooming.
*   **Emotion:** Immersion, focus, entering a new world.

## Sequence 4: Shutdown (The Sleep)
*   **Camera:** Orbits slowly around the Living Orb, drifting slightly upward.
*   **Lighting/Animation:** The Orb's internal turbulence slows down. The emissive core dims from white to deep blue, then finally collapses into a single pixel before winking out.
*   **Emotion:** Rest, finality, safety.
