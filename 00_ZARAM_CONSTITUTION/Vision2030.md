# Zaram Vision 2030

> **Zaram is a Personal Intelligence Operating System that allows people to own, grow, and embody their intelligence across any device, model, or digital form.**

## Core Philosophy
- **Local-First Sovereignty:** The user owns their models, memories, data, and workflows. No subscriptions required. No internet required. No vendor lock-in.
- **Semantic Physics:** Intelligence is not static. It has gravity. Important, fresh, and highly connected knowledge naturally pulls toward the center. Forgotten knowledge drifts to the edges.
- **Embodiment Agnosticism:** The AI's intelligence is entirely decoupled from its body. The same mind can inhabit a Living Orb, a MetaHuman, a Robot, or a Spatial Universe without rewriting a single line of logic.
- **Platform, Not Product:** Zaram is the foundation. The ecosystem (Marketplace, Providers, Renderers) is built by the community.
