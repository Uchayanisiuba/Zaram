## 16. The User Promise & Business Philosophy
> **Your intelligence is yours. Zaram never locks your hardware behind a subscription.**

Zaram Core is a one-time purchase (Recommended: $49). It includes a lifetime license, unlimited local AI, unlimited orchestration, unlimited local agents, unlimited local memory, and lifetime updates. 

Cloud models (GPT, Claude, Gemini) are strictly optional. Users only pay via credits if they explicitly choose to route a task to a cloud provider. Local users never lose functionality.

The Marketplace allows third-party developers to publish Skills, Characters, Voice Packs, and Knowledge Packs. Revenue comes from the ecosystem, not from limiting base intelligence.
