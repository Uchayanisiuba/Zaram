2. **The Marketplace:** Split into three distinct storefronts:

   ### Developer Store
   - SDKs & APIs
   - Plugins & Extensions
   - Developer Tools
   - Integration Packs

   ### Creator Store
   - **Embodiments:** MetaHuman, RPM, VRM, Custom Characters, Robots, Floating AI
   - **Characters:** Fully realized digital personalities
   - **Voices:** TTS voice packs, voice clones
   - **Themes:** Orb themes, Avatar themes, **Galaxy themes**, Universe configurations
   - **Knowledge Packs:** Curated knowledge bases, research collections
   - **Memory Packs:** Pre-configured memory templates
   - **Workspace Templates:** Project layouts, workflow configurations
   - **Animations:** Motion packs, gesture libraries, orbital paths
   - **Skills:** Task-specific capabilities
   - **Plugins:** Third-party integrations

   ### Enterprise Store
   - **Industry Packs:** Medical, Legal, Finance, Education, Engineering
   - **Compliance Packs:** HIPAA, GDPR, SOC2 templates
   - **Security Templates:** Encrypted workspaces, audit logs
   - **Deployment Packs:** On-premise installation, team management
