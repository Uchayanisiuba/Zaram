# Memory — what Zaram remembers, and how to correct it

## What goes in

Zaram remembers facts about you and your work: a client's rate, a decision you made, how you like things written. Facts come from three places, and each fact says which — a document of yours, something you said in conversation, or something Zaram itself generated. A fact from a document Zaram wrote is trusted less than one from a document you gave it.

Conversation itself is not memory. What you say in passing stays with that conversation; a fact enters memory when Zaram judges it worth keeping, or when you press **Remember** under a reply.

## Seeing what it knows

![Memory: facts with their source, and the commitments Zaram found.](assets/memory.png)

Open **Memory** from the landing. Facts are listed with their source and when they were last used. Search the box at the top to find one.

## Correcting a fact

Click a fact and edit it. Every answer that used the old version changes from then on. This is the most important control in Zaram: it is how it learns what is true rather than what it once heard.

## Deleting a fact

Click a fact and choose delete. It is gone, and answers stop using it. Nothing Zaram remembers is beyond your reach.

## Global and project facts

A fact is either about **you** — your preferences, your way of writing — or about **a project** — a client's terms, a decision on one job. Project facts show only when that project is open; global facts show always. When a fact is recalled across several projects, Zaram may ask whether it is really about you and should become global.

## Commitments

Memory also holds the **commitments** Zaram found in your documents: a payment due in 30 days, a delivery by the 14th, a quote that expires. Each shows the sentence it came from, so you can check it. You can correct the date, mark it met, or dismiss it. **Export to calendar** writes them as a calendar file you can open in whatever calendar you keep.

When something falls due within a week, Zaram tells you once — a small notification from the desktop — and never nags.

## Retention

A permanent record of every question you have ever asked is a privacy problem of its own. **Activity** lets you set how long conversations are kept, and prune them. What is pruned is recorded too.

## Export — taking your memory with you

To export your memory, open **Settings → Export**. The export is a file in an open format that holds everything Zaram remembers, so you can take your memory with you, keep a copy, or move it to another machine. Nothing is locked to Zaram.
